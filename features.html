A warm house first.

Ai-Neut opens on what's already going right. The planning and the harder stuff are still there, on the porch, so opening the app doesn't put the hard part in your hand.

Try it free on the web

Home

Starts from a house that's already warm.

  • A clean joke for the table
  • Tonight's keep: a story, homework, or lights-out
  • One clean next step, like “I'll take bedtime”
  • One ordinary good moment, saved for a flat day
  • A 90-day strip that counts only what was kept. Empty days are just paper, not a failure chart.

Note

Your private family journal.

  • One true good thing, to remember
  • Before a harder sentence: “Will the kid feel this?”
  • Three ways to say it: warm, direct, your voice
  • Nothing sends itself

Laugh

Fun is maintenance.

  • Table jokes and family bits
  • A slapstick stack nobody gets hurt by
  • An adults-only corner that stays hidden until you tap Show, and never shows on a kid-facing screen

Grow

Small steps for kids and grown-ups.

  • A word for dinner, ten minutes of play, a feeling word, one finished small thing
  • A week of reading, outside, making, helping, and someone else
  • No grades, no comparison, no leaderboard

Porch

The same roof, for the planning side.

  • This week's Sunday check-in and a midweek check-in
  • Shared calendar, ride and childcare swaps
  • A calmer way to word a message
  • House standards that read as kindness
  • A small, private circle of trusted people

What Ai-Neut is not

No scores, no grades, no diagnosis, no public profiles, and no tool for keeping score between adults. Single, blended, or co-parenting, every family gets the same front door.

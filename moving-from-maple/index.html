Moving from Maple

A Maple alternative for family plans

Maple’s notice says Maple has been acquired by Wander and will sunset on December 31, 2026. Your data stays accessible until then. Once Maple shuts down, all of that data is deleted and cannot be recovered.

The core app is free, with no ads

The core app is free, with no ads. It includes a shared calendar, a way to share free time rather than a whole calendar, a private journal, and chores with a backlog.

Export from Maple, then bring your calendar

  1. Follow Maple’s export instructions. Maple’s notice says: “We highly recommend taking this opportunity to export or transfer your data to other apps or services before Maple shuts down.” Those public pages do not list the clicks.
  2. Sign in with Google and connect Google Calendar. In the app, that is “Sign in with Google and tap Allow.” Google Calendar sign-in is open to testers while Google’s review finishes.

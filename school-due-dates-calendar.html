See every school deadline before Sunday at 11:59 PM

Online classes love one deadline: Sunday, 11:59 PM. Ten of them on one night turn a calendar into a wall of text. Ai-Neut's Due view lists them, grouped by day, so high school and college students, and the parents keeping up with them, can see the week at a glance. Teens 13 and up can use Ai-Neut on their own. No ads, ever.

Add a school calendar

Works with your school's calendar link

  • D2L Brightspace, Canvas, Blackboard, or any class or team calendar that gives you an ICS / webcal link
  • Open Calendar settings, choose School or other link, and paste it. The full address stays hidden
  • It refreshes on its own

A Due list that reads like a checklist

  • Quizzes, exams, discussions, and assignments land in Due automatically
  • Grouped by day, with each course in its own color
  • A late-night pile-up shows as a short list, not stacked boxes at the bottom of Sunday
  • Mark items done, and hide or show finished ones

Reminders at the times that help

  • Due reminders the day before and the morning of
  • An optional Friday-evening and Sunday-afternoon nudge, before the weekend gets away
  • Ask by voice: “What's due this week?”

How to find your school calendar link

D2L Brightspace

Open Calendar in Brightspace, click Settings, check Enable Calendar Feeds and save. Then click Subscribe, choose All Calendars and Tasks, and copy the link. Your school may name things a little differently. Full D2L guide, with Google, Outlook, and Apple steps →

Canvas

Open Calendar in Canvas and click Calendar Feed, then copy the link.

Blackboard and others

Look for “calendar feed,” “subscribe,” “iCal,” or “export” on the calendar page. Any link ending in .ics or starting with webcal:// works.

Tip: the link is private to the student. Paste it only into your own family's Ai-Neut, and reset it in the school system if it gets shared.

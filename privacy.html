What stays on your device

These are saved in your browser's or app's storage on your phone or computer. We don't receive them:

  • Your journal, private notes, moods, marks, stickers and drawings, plus the journal lock code and master code.
  • Your personal calendar, reminders, to-dos, lists, birthdays, chores, points and settings (theme, voice, profile, birth year).
  • Google Calendar or Outlook sign-in tokens, if you connect them, and a short cache of those events.
  • If you turn on "Help improve voice," the list of what you asked the mic and whether it worked.
  • A small flag that remembers you're using the Android app.

Microphone: the mic button uses your device's or browser's own speech recognition to turn speech into text. On many Android phones and in Chrome, that service is run by Google under Google's own policies. Sαréal618 gets only the text and never records or uploads audio. Sαréal618 doesn't send your words to an AI model.

Save a file on this phone (Settings) downloads a copy (Sαréal618.json) to your device anytime. That copy is yours, and we don't receive it.

Your plan, in your own Google Drive

If you connect Google, your plan (calendar items, lists, birthdays, chores and points) is also kept in one hidden file in a Sαréal618 app folder in your own Google Drive, so it shows up on your other devices, including a computer after you sign in. Your device reads and writes that file directly with Google. It doesn't pass through our server, and we never receive it.

  • We can't see your other Drive files, photos or email.
  • The file counts toward your Google storage like any other app data (it's small).
  • Remove it anytime: Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps → Sαréal618 → Delete hidden app data, and remove Sαréal618's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Calendar items you share with your family are locked (encrypted) on your phone with the family key before they sync, and our server can't read them.

Google permissions, in plain words

Before Google shows its own permission page, Sαréal618 tells you what each permission is for. Here is the same list:

  • Sign in with Google: shares your name, email and Google account ID with us so you have an account. Sαréal618 has no passwords. It doesn't open your Drive, photos, email or calendar.
  • Google Drive, app folder only (drive.appdata): saves one hidden copy of your plan in your own Google Drive, so it follows you to your other devices. We can't see, open or change your other Drive files, your photos or your email.
  • Google Calendar (calendar.events, calendar.calendarlist.readonly): only if you tap Connect Google Calendar. The calendar list is used to show which of your calendars to bring in, by name and color, so you can turn each one on or off. Event access is used to show the events from the calendars you turn on in your Sαréal618 plan, and to add, change or delete an event in your main Google Calendar only when you ask. We confirm before deleting. It can't change your calendars' settings or sharing, or create or delete whole calendars.

Connecting Drive and Calendar is optional and separate from signing in. Your device talks directly to Google for both, and Google tokens stay on your device and are never sent to our server. Your plan, with the event copies in it, syncs through the hidden file in your own Google Drive, not through our server. Two exceptions, only if you use them: in a linked family the shared items are sealed (end-to-end encrypted) on our server, and a calendar link you publish is kept on our server so other calendar apps can read it (see What our server still keeps). Google data is never sent to any AI and never used for ads. Remove access anytime in your Google account settings. Microsoft Outlook works the same way (account.live.com/consent/Manage).

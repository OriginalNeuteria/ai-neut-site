Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 24, 2026. This policy covers the Ai-Neut web app at app.ai-neut.com, the Ai-Neut Android app (package com.aineut.app, which opens that same web app), and this website, www.ai-neut.com.

Ai-Neut is a family app for parents, teens, and kids. Kids under 13 join only after a parent says yes by email, then links the kid's phone with a one-time code. Parents are the safety checkpoint: they write the shop's rewards and approve what kids ask for. A kid's journal stays on that kid's phone.

The short version

  • No ads. Ever, for anyone. No ad networks, no advertising ID, no ad tracking, and no analytics trackers.
  • We don't sell or rent anyone's data, and we don't build profiles of people.
  • Most of what you put in Ai-Neut (journal, notes, drawings, your calendar) stays on your device. Family items you share are encrypted on the phone before they sync.
  • Kids under 13 join only after a parent says yes by email and links the kid's phone with a one-time code. Kids never sign in, can't buy anything, can't talk to anyone outside the family, and the AI is off for them.
  • Teens 13 to 17 can use Ai-Neut on their own, with privacy-protective defaults and no adult rooms.
  • The core app is free. Helping Hand is an optional paid upgrade ($36 a year), bought on our website. It isn't sold inside the Android app.
  • You can delete everything from inside the app, or by emailing us. Parents can delete a kid's data at any time. See Delete account.

Kids' privacy (COPPA notice)

This section is our notice to parents under the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). It applies to kids under 13.

What we collect from or about a kid, and why

  • From the parent, when setting up the kid: a nickname (it doesn't need to be the real name), an age band, the parent's own email address (for the consent letters), and a 4-number parent code. This is used to show the kid's profile and chores in the family and to prove the parent said yes.
  • From the kid's use: chore check-offs, points, reward asks in the points shop, shared family calendar items and lists, and short notes to linked parents. These sync to the family so the parent can see and approve them. Anything the kid writes in their journal, notes, or drawings stays on the kid's phone and isn't sent to us.
  • Technical data: a random profile ID and the private token for the family link, used only to keep the app working and secure (what COPPA calls "support for internal operations"). It's never used for ads or profiling.

Family data that syncs, including the kid's profile, chores, and points, is end-to-end encrypted with the family key on the phone. Our server stores only the locked copy and can't read it.

What we never do with a kid's information

  • No ads, behavioral advertising, profiling, or selling, and no sharing with third parties.
  • No AI: a kid's profile never sends anything to the AI model, and kids' information is never used to train AI.
  • No sign-in of any kind, no email address or phone number from the kid, no location, and no photos or voice recordings uploaded. The mic's "Help improve voice" option isn't shown on a kid's profile.
  • No public posts, profiles, or chat, and no way to contact or be contacted by anyone outside the family.
  • No purchases. The points shop uses only rewards the parent writes in (like "ice cream" or "pick the movie"). Points have no cash value and can't buy Helping Hand or anything with real money.
  • No YouTube embeds or links to outside websites inside a kid's profile without a parent gate.
  • No home screen widgets on a kid's phone.

How parent consent works

On the kid's phone, a parent (18+) enters the kid's nickname, the parent's own email, and a 4-number parent code, then taps Email the parent a yes link. We email the parent a letter from support@ai-neut.com. That letter is our direct notice: it says what Ai-Neut keeps for a kid, that none of it is sold, used for ads, or sent to an AI, and how to review or delete it. No kid profile and no phone code exist until the parent opens the link and taps Yes. We then send a second letter (the FTC's "email plus" method), and the one-time phone code appears only after the parent confirms that second letter. The code works once, for 15 minutes. If the parent taps No, nothing is turned on and the request is deleted right away. A request nobody answers is deleted after 48 hours.

What a kid can do after consent: chores and chore points, the points shop, the shared family calendar and lists, a journal that stays on the kid's phone, and short notes to linked parents.

Parents' rights

A parent can, at any time, review a kid's synced information (not the on-device journal, which we never have), stop further collection, withdraw consent, or delete the kid's information. Do this in Family in the app, or email support@ai-neut.com. We'll ask a question to confirm you're the parent. Withdrawing consent deletes the kid's synced information on the schedule below.

We don't make a kid give more information than the activity needs. If we learn we've collected a kid's personal information without parent consent, we delete it.

How long we keep kids' information

We keep a kid's information only as long as we need it to run the family features the parent turned on. A consent request that isn't answered is deleted after 48 hours, or right away if the parent says No. When a parent removes a kid or deletes the family, the kid's synced information (chores, points, shop asks, shared calendar items and family notes) is deleted within 30 days, including from backups. If a kid link has no activity for 12 months, we email the parent once, then delete the kid's synced information 30 days later unless the parent asks us to keep it. A kid's journal never leaves the kid's phone, so we never have it. We keep a record that consent was given (the parent's email and the date) for as long as the kid's profile exists, plus 3 years, to show we got consent.

Teens (13 to 17)

  • Teens can use Ai-Neut on their own, and may sign in. Using the app doesn't need a parent's permission.
  • Teen defaults: adult rooms are never available; the journal stays on the phone; nothing is public; there are no ads, no profiling, and no selling.
  • Helping Hand (AI) opens for a teen only after a linked parent says yes on the parent's own phone, and it uses stricter safety settings (see Helping Hand and AI).
  • Linking to a family is optional. A teen can join a family with a code and leave it anytime. A linked parent sees only the shared family items (calendar, lists, chores, points), never the teen's journal or notes.
  • Texas: under the Texas App Store Accountability Act, Google Play verifies ages and gets a parent's consent for minors who download apps in Texas. Ai-Neut uses the age and consent information Google Play provides only to apply the right profile, and deletes it once checked.

What stays on your device

These are saved in your browser's or app's local storage on your phone or computer. We don't receive them:

  • Journal, private notes, moods, marks, stickers, and drawings, plus the journal lock code and master code.
  • Your personal calendar, reminders, to-dos, birthdays, and settings (theme, voice, profile, birth year, parent code).
  • Google Calendar or Outlook sign-in tokens, if you connect them, and a short cache of those events.

Microphone: the mic button uses your device's or browser's own speech recognition to turn speech into text. On many Android phones and in Chrome, that service is run by Google under Google's own policies. Ai-Neut gets only the text and never records or uploads audio. Front camera: some themes may use the camera only as a light sensor, reading tiny frames on the device. Nothing is saved or uploaded. Both need your permission.

Save a file on this phone downloads a copy (Ai-Neut.json) to your device. That copy is yours, and we don't receive it.

The Android app and home screen widgets

  • The Ai-Neut Android app opens the same web app at app.ai-neut.com, so everything in this policy applies to it the same way. It has no ads SDK and doesn't use the advertising ID.
  • Widgets: home screen widgets keep a short copy of your upcoming items on your phone so they can show them. If you're signed in, the phone also keeps a private widget token, used only to fetch your own items from Ai-Neut. The token is replaced every 30 days. Removing the widgets, tapping Disconnect in widget settings, or using Delete my data deletes the token and the copy on the phone. Widgets aren't available on kids' phones.
  • Helping Hand isn't sold inside the Android app. If you want it, you can buy it on our website.

Google Calendar and Microsoft Outlook (adults and teens)

  • Connecting a calendar is optional, separate from signing in, and not available on kids' profiles. The app explains what it will access before you connect.
  • Google Calendar: Ai-Neut can view and edit events on your calendars (scope calendar.events) and see your calendar list (calendar.calendarlist.readonly). It never asks for full calendar access (the broader calendar scope), so it can't change your calendars' settings, sharing, or create or delete whole calendars. Microsoft Outlook / Microsoft 365 asks for Calendars.ReadWrite, User.Read, and offline_access.
  • We use calendar access only to show your events and to add, change, or delete events when you ask, and we confirm before deleting.
  • Your device talks directly to Google or Microsoft. Events and tokens stay on your device. They aren't sent to our server, aren't sent to the AI unless you include a specific event in an ask, and are never used for ads.
  • Disconnect any time in Calendar settings, or at myaccount.google.com/permissions or account.live.com/consent/Manage.

Google user data: Limited Use

Ai-Neut's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. In particular:

  • Google Calendar data is used only for the calendar features you can see in the app.
  • It's not transferred, except to provide those features with your consent, for security, to comply with the law, or in a merger or sale (such as the move to an LLC) after asking for your consent first.
  • It's not sold, not used for ads, and not used for credit or lending decisions.
  • No person reads it unless you ask us to, it's needed for security or the law, or it's aggregated for internal operations.
  • It's not used to develop, improve, or train general AI or machine-learning models.

Microsoft data gets the same treatment.

Helping Hand and AI (adults and teens only)

Helping Hand is the optional paid part of Ai-Neut ($36 a year; the core app is free). It uses an AI model from our AI service provider to answer asks, make pictures, plan a "hunt" for your town, and speak in the named voices. When you use it, what you type or say, any picture you add, and the text to be spoken are sent to that provider so it can produce the answer. The provider processes this for us as a service provider. It isn't used for ads or sold.

  • Never on kids' profiles. Teen profiles need a parent's yes first and use stricter safety instructions: no romantic or sexual content, no dangerous challenges, and crisis resources whenever self-harm or abuse comes up.
  • The journal, calendar, chores, points shop, and the rest of the free app don't call the AI.
  • Helping Hand is bought on our website, with Stripe. It isn't sold inside the Android app.
  • AI answers can be wrong. Report anything offensive or wrong with the Report button on the answer (see Safety).

Who else gets data

We don't sell, rent, or trade personal information, we don't share it for advertising, and Ai-Neut has no ads. Kids' information isn't disclosed to any third party. We use only these service providers, and only to run Ai-Neut:

  • App hosting: our app-hosting provider runs app.ai-neut.com and stores the server data above. Family data is stored only in encrypted form.
  • Website hosting: GitHub Pages and Cloudflare.
  • Sign-in: Google, for adults and teens who choose to sign in.
  • Calendars: Google and Microsoft, directly from your device.
  • AI: our AI service provider, for Helping Hand (adults and teens).
  • Payments: Stripe, on our website (adults).
  • Reminders: your phone's or browser's push service (such as Google's), which delivers the encrypted reminder.
  • Email: Google (Gmail), for messages and consent letters.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, to protect someone's safety, or to stop fraud or abuse. If Ai-Neut is transferred, including to Charles's LLC, the new owner must follow this policy.

Deleting your data

In the app: Settings → Delete my data erases that device's Ai-Neut data, including widget data. If you're signed in, Delete my account and synced data also deletes your account and server data. Parents can delete a kid's information in Family, or by emailing us. On the web, or without the app: see www.ai-neut.com/delete-account.html. We finish web requests within 30 days.

Your rights

  • Use Ai-Neut without an account, turn off any connection, and delete your data at any time.
  • Email us to get a copy of your data, or to correct or delete it. We answer within 30 days, and you won't be treated differently for asking.
  • Ai-Neut is offered to users in the United States. We give these rights to everyone, whether or not a state's privacy law applies to us.

Security

All connections use HTTPS. Family data is end-to-end encrypted with the family key. Calendar tokens stay on the device. We keep a written security program for kids' data, limit who can access our systems, and delete data on the schedule above. If a breach affects your information, we'll tell you as the law requires.

Changes to this policy

We'll update the effective date when this policy changes, and give notice in the app before important changes (including the move to an LLC). We won't use data we already have in a new way without asking first. For kids, that means new parent consent. We tell Google Play about significant changes as Texas law requires.

Kids' privacy (COPPA notice)

This section is our notice to parents under the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). It applies to kids under 13 on the Sαréal618 web app. The Android app is for ages 13 and up and doesn't offer kid profiles.

What we collect from or about a kid, and why

  • From the parent, when setting up the kid: a nickname (it doesn't need to be the real name), an age band, the parent's own email address (for the consent letters), and a 4-number parent code. This is used to show the kid's profile and chores in the family and to prove the parent said yes.
  • From the kid's use: chore check-offs, points, reward asks in the points shop, shared family calendar items and lists, and short notes to linked parents. These sync to the family so the parent can see and approve them. Anything the kid writes in their journal, notes, or drawings stays on the kid's phone and isn't sent to us.
  • Technical data: a random profile ID and the private token for the family link, used only to keep the app working and secure (what COPPA calls "support for internal operations"). It's never used for ads or profiling.

Family data that syncs, including the kid's profile, chores, and points, is end-to-end encrypted with the family key on the phone. Our server stores only the locked copy and can't read it.

Schools: Sαréal618 is a family app. It isn't made for schools, and we don't have agreements with schools. Families can type school dates into their own calendar.

What we never do with a kid's information

  • No ads, behavioral advertising, profiling, or selling, and no sharing with third parties.
  • No AI: a kid's profile never sends anything to an AI model, and kids' information is never used to train AI.
  • No sign-in of any kind, no email address or phone number from the kid, no location, and no photos or voice recordings uploaded. The mic's "Help improve voice" option isn't shown on a kid's profile.
  • No public posts, profiles, or chat, and no way to contact or be contacted by anyone outside the family.
  • No purchases. The points shop uses only rewards the parent writes in (like "ice cream" or "pick the movie"). Points have no cash value.
  • No YouTube embeds or links to outside websites inside a kid's profile without a parent gate.
  • No home screen widgets on a kid's phone.

How parent consent works

On the kid's phone, in the web app, a parent (18+) enters the kid's nickname, the parent's own email, and a 4-number parent code, then taps Email the parent a yes link. We email the parent a letter from Sαréal618. That letter is our direct notice: it says what Sαréal618 keeps for a kid, that none of it is sold, used for ads, or sent to an AI, and how to review or delete it. No kid profile and no phone code exist until the parent opens the link and taps Yes. We then send a second letter (the FTC's "email plus" method), and the one-time phone code appears only after the parent confirms that second letter. The code works once, for 15 minutes. If the parent taps No, nothing is turned on and the request is deleted right away. Every request, answered or not, is deleted 48 hours after it was made.

When a parent says yes, we keep a consent record: a scrambled (hashed) copy of the parent's email, the kid's nickname, the date, and the method ("email plus"). It doesn't hold the plain email address. It's how we show that a parent said yes.

What a kid can do after consent: chores and chore points, the points shop, the shared family calendar and lists, a journal that stays on the kid's phone, and short notes to linked parents.

Parents' rights

A parent can, at any time, review a kid's synced information (not the on-device journal, which we never have), stop further collection, withdraw consent, or delete the kid's information. Signed-in parents can remove one kid in Settings → Linked kids: that deletes the kid's link, the kid phone's sync access, and the consent record right away, and the kid's phone stops syncing. The kid's items inside the encrypted family board are removed from the parent's phone, because only family phones can read them. You can also email support. We'll ask a question to confirm you're the parent.

We don't make a kid give more information than the activity needs. If we learn we've collected a kid's personal information without parent consent, we delete it.

How long we keep each kind of kid information is listed in our written data retention policy below.

What stays on your device

These are saved in your browser's or app's local storage on your phone or computer. We don't receive them:

  • Journal, private notes, moods, marks, stickers, and drawings, plus the journal lock code and master code.
  • Your personal calendar, reminders, to-dos, birthdays, and settings (theme, voice, profile, birth year, parent code).
  • Google Calendar or Outlook sign-in tokens, if you connect them, and a short cache of those events.
  • If you turn on "Help improve voice," the list of what you asked the mic and whether it worked. Right now that list stays on your phone. Our server doesn't keep any of it.
  • A small flag that remembers you're using the Android app, so the app keeps its 13+ setup.

Microphone: the mic button uses your device's or browser's own speech recognition to turn speech into text. On many Android phones and in Chrome, that service is run by Google under Google's own policies. Sαréal618 gets only the text and never records or uploads audio. Front camera: some themes may use the camera only as a light sensor, reading tiny frames on the device. Nothing is saved or uploaded. Both need your permission.

Save a file on this phone downloads a copy (Sαréal618.json) to your device. That copy is yours, and we don't receive it.

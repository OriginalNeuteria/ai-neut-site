Teens (13 to 17)

  • Teens can use Sαréal618 on their own, including the Android app, and may sign in. Using the app doesn't need a parent's permission.
  • Teen defaults: adult rooms are never available; the journal stays on the phone; nothing is public; there are no ads, no profiling, and no selling.
  • Linking to a family is optional. A teen can join a family with a code and leave it anytime. A linked parent sees only the shared family items (calendar, lists, chores, points), never the teen's journal or notes.
  • Texas: under the Texas App Store Accountability Act, Google Play verifies ages and gets a parent's consent for minors who download apps in Texas. If Sαréal618 receives age or consent information from Google Play, it's used only to apply the right profile and is deleted once checked.

What stays on your device

These are saved in your browser's or app's local storage on your phone or computer. We don't receive them:

  • Journal, private notes, moods, marks, stickers, and drawings, plus the journal lock code and master code.
  • Your personal calendar, reminders, to-dos, birthdays, and settings (theme, voice, profile, and birth year).
  • Google Calendar or Outlook sign-in tokens, if you connect them, and a short cache of those events.
  • If you turn on "Help improve voice," the list of what you asked the mic and whether it worked. Right now that list stays on your phone. Our server doesn't keep any of it.
  • A small flag that remembers you're using the Android app, so the app keeps its 13+ setup.

Microphone: the mic button uses your device's or browser's own speech recognition to turn speech into text. On many Android phones and in Chrome, that service is run by Google under Google's own policies. Sαréal618 gets only the text and never records or uploads audio. Front camera: some themes may use the camera only as a light sensor, reading tiny frames on the device. Nothing is saved or uploaded. Both need your permission.

Save a file on this phone downloads a copy (Sαréal618.json) to your device. That copy is yours, and we don't receive it.

Google Calendar and Microsoft Outlook (adults and teens)

  • Connecting a calendar is optional, separate from signing in, and only for people 13 and up. The app explains what it will access before you connect.
  • Google Calendar: Sαréal618 can view and edit events on your calendars (scope calendar.events) and see your calendar list (calendar.calendarlist.readonly). It never asks for full calendar access (the broader calendar scope), so it can't change your calendars' settings, sharing, or create or delete whole calendars. Microsoft Outlook / Microsoft 365 asks for Calendars.ReadWrite, User.Read, and offline_access.
  • We use calendar access only to show your events and to add, change, or delete events when you ask, and we confirm before deleting.
  • Your device talks directly to Google or Microsoft. Events and tokens stay on your device. They aren't sent to our server, aren't sent to any AI, and are never used for ads.
  • Disconnect any time in Calendar settings, or at myaccount.google.com/permissions or account.live.com/consent/Manage.

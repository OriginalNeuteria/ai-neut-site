Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 17, 2026. This policy covers the Ai-Neut web app at ai-neut.grok.me, the Ai-Neut Android app (package com.aineut.app), and this website, www.ai-neut.com.

Ai-Neut is a family app for parents, teens, and kids. Family profiles, parent consent for kids under 13, the points-shop approvals, and the in-app Report button are rolling out in the app update that comes before the Google Play launch. Items marked rolling out describe that update. Until it arrives, a parent should set up any phone a child under 13 uses, in kid's phone mode, which keeps the child's writing on that phone.

The short version

  • No ads. Ever, for anyone. No ad networks, no advertising ID, no ad tracking, and no analytics trackers.
  • We don't sell or rent anyone's data, and we don't build profiles of people.
  • Most of what you put in Ai-Neut (journal, notes, drawings, your calendar) stays on your device. Family items you share are encrypted on the phone before they sync.
  • Kids under 13 join only after a parent adds them and gives consent. Kids don't sign in with Google or X, can't buy anything, can't talk to strangers, and the AI is off for them.
  • Teens 13 to 17 can use Ai-Neut on their own, with privacy-protective defaults and no adult rooms.
  • You can delete everything from inside the app, or by emailing us. Parents can delete a child's data at any time. See Delete account.

Children's privacy (COPPA notice)

This section is our notice to parents under the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). It applies to children under 13.

What we collect from or about a child, and why

  • From the parent, when setting up the child's profile: a nickname or first name (it doesn't need to be the real name), an age band (6 to 8 or 9 to 12), and an avatar or color. This is used to show the child's profile and chores in the family.
  • From the child's use: chore check-offs, points, and reward requests in the points shop. These sync to the family so the parent can see and approve them. Anything the child writes in their journal, notes, or drawings stays on the child's phone and isn't sent to us.
  • Technical data: a random device/profile ID and the sign-in token for the family link, used only to keep the app working and secure (what COPPA calls "support for internal operations"). It's never used for ads or profiling.

Family data that syncs, including the child's profile, chores, and points, is end-to-end encrypted with the family key on the phone. Our server stores only the locked copy and can't read it.

What we never do with a child's information

  • No ads, behavioral advertising, profiling, or selling, and no sharing with third parties.
  • No AI: a kid's profile never sends anything to the AI model.
  • No sign-in with Google or X, no email address or phone number from the child, no location, no photos or voice recordings uploaded.
  • No public posts, profiles, or chat, and no way to contact or be contacted by anyone outside the family.
  • No purchases. The points shop uses only rewards the parent writes in (like "ice cream" or "pick the movie"). Points have no cash value and can't buy anything in the app.
  • No YouTube embeds or links to outside websites inside a kid's profile.

Parental consent

Before a child's profile works, the parent (an adult with a signed-in parent account) reads this notice in the app and confirms consent, and we email the parent's address a direct notice with a consent link. Because we use children's information only internally and don't disclose it to third parties, we use the FTC's "email plus" method: after consent, we send a second confirmation email that repeats this notice and explains how to withdraw consent. If consent isn't confirmed, the child's profile isn't activated, and anything entered for it is deleted within 14 days.

Parents' rights

A parent can, at any time: review the information in the child's profile (Family → the child → See this child's data); change it; stop further collection; withdraw consent; or delete the child's profile and data (Family → the child → Delete this child), or email familyainewt@gmail.com. We'll ask a question to confirm you're the parent. Withdrawing consent deletes the child's profile and synced data. The child's on-device journal is erased when the child's phone is unlinked or the app's data is cleared.

We don't make a child give more information than the activity needs. If we learn we've collected a child's personal information without parental consent, we delete it.

Teens (13 to 17)

  • Teens can use Ai-Neut on their own and may sign in with Google or X. Ai-Neut doesn't ask for a parent's permission.
  • Teen defaults: adult rooms are never available; the journal stays on the phone; nothing is public; there are no ads, no profiling, and no selling.
  • Helping Hand (AI) is available to teens, with stricter safety settings (see Helping Hand and AI). Teens can buy Helping Hand only through Google Play, which handles any parent approvals (Family Link, and the Texas law below).
  • Linking to a family is optional. A teen can join a family with a code and leave it anytime. A linked parent sees only the shared family items (calendar, lists, chores, points), never the teen's journal or notes. If a parent account in the family turns off Helping Hand for the teen, the setting shows on the teen's phone.
  • Texas: under the Texas App Store Accountability Act, Google Play verifies ages and gets a parent's consent for minors who download apps or make purchases in Texas. Ai-Neut uses the age and consent information Google Play provides only to apply the right profile, and deletes it once checked.

What stays on your device

These are saved in your browser's or app's local storage on your phone or computer. We don't receive them:

  • Journal, private notes, moods, marks, stickers, and drawings, plus the journal lock code and master code.
  • Your personal calendar, reminders, to-dos, birthdays, and settings (theme, voice, profile, parent code).
  • Google Calendar or Outlook sign-in tokens, if you connect them, and a short cache of those events.

Microphone: the Ask/mic button uses your device's or browser's speech recognition to turn speech into text. On many Android phones and in Chrome, that service is run by Google under Google's own policies. Ai-Neut gets only the text and doesn't record or upload audio. Front camera: some themes may use the camera only as a light sensor, reading tiny frames on the device. Nothing is saved or uploaded. Both need your permission.

Save a file on this phone downloads a copy (Ai-Neut.json) to your device. That copy is yours, and we don't receive it.

Google Calendar and Microsoft Outlook (adults and teens)

  • Connecting a calendar is optional, separate from signing in, and not available on kids' profiles. The app explains what it will access before you connect.
  • Google Calendar asks to see and edit your calendar events (scope calendar.events) and to see your list of calendars (calendar.calendarlist.readonly). Until the next app update narrows this, it may ask for the broader calendar scope. Microsoft Outlook / Microsoft 365 asks for Calendars.ReadWrite, User.Read, and offline_access.
  • We use calendar access only to show your events and to add, change, or delete events when you ask, and we confirm before deleting.
  • Your device talks directly to Google or Microsoft. Events and tokens stay on your device. They aren't sent to our server, aren't sent to the AI unless you include a specific event in an ask, and are never used for ads.
  • Disconnect any time in Calendar settings, or at myaccount.google.com/permissions or account.live.com/consent/Manage.

Google user data: Limited Use

Ai-Neut's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. In particular:

  • Google Calendar data is used only for the calendar features you can see in the app.
  • It's not transferred, except to provide those features with your consent, for security, to comply with the law, or in a merger or sale (such as the move to an LLC) after asking for your consent first.
  • It's not sold, not used for ads, and not used for credit or lending decisions.
  • No person reads it unless you ask us to, it's needed for security or the law, or it's aggregated for internal operations.
  • It's not used to develop, improve, or train general AI or machine-learning models.

Microsoft data gets the same treatment.

Helping Hand and AI (adults and teens only)

Helping Hand is the optional paid part of Ai-Neut that uses an AI model (Grok, from xAI) to answer asks, make pictures, plan a "hunt" for your town, and speak in the named voices (Luna, Ara, Ursa, Eve). When you use it, what you type or say, any picture you add, and the text to be spoken are sent to xAI so it can produce the answer. xAI processes this as our service provider under its own policies. It isn't used for ads or sold.

  • Never on kids' profiles. Teen profiles use stricter safety instructions: no romantic or sexual content, no dangerous challenges, and crisis resources whenever self-harm or abuse comes up.
  • The journal, calendar, chores, points shop, and the rest of the free app don't call the AI.
  • AI answers can be wrong. Report anything offensive or wrong with the in-app Report button (rolling out; see Safety).

Who else gets data

We don't sell, rent, or trade personal information, we don't share it for advertising, and Ai-Neut has no ads. Children's information isn't disclosed to any third party. We use only these service providers, and only to run Ai-Neut:

  • App hosting: the Grok app-builder platform that runs ai-neut.grok.me and stores the server data above. Family data is stored only in encrypted form.
  • Website hosting: GitHub Pages and Cloudflare.
  • Sign-in: Google and X, for adults and teens who choose them.
  • Calendars: Google and Microsoft, directly from your device.
  • AI: xAI, for Helping Hand (adults and teens).
  • Payments: Stripe on the web (adults), and Google Play in the Android app.
  • Email: Google (Gmail), for messages and consent emails.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, to protect someone's safety, or to stop fraud or abuse. If Ai-Neut is transferred, including to Charles's LLC, the new owner must follow this policy.

Deleting your data

In the app: Settings → Delete my data erases that device's data and, if you're signed in, your account and server data. Parents: Family → the child → Delete this child. On the web, or without the app: see Delete account. We finish web requests within 30 days.

Your rights

  • Use Ai-Neut without an account, turn off any connection, and delete your data at any time.
  • Email us to get a copy of your data, or to correct or delete it. We answer within 30 days, and you won't be treated differently for asking.
  • Ai-Neut is offered to users in the United States. We give these rights to everyone, whether or not a state's privacy law applies to us.

Security

All connections use HTTPS. Family data is end-to-end encrypted with the family key. Calendar tokens stay on the device. We keep a written security program for children's data, limit who can access our systems, and delete data on the schedule above. If a breach affects your information, we'll tell you as the law requires.

Changes to this policy

We'll update the effective date when this policy changes, and give notice in the app before important changes (including the move to an LLC). We won't use data we already have in a new way without asking first. For children, that means new parental consent. We tell Google Play about significant changes as Texas law requires.

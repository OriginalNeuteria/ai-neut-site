The short version

  • Ai-Neut is made for adults 18 and over: parents, guardians, and other grown-ups who run a household.
  • Most of what you put in Ai-Neut (journal, notes, drawings, your calendar) stays on your device. You don't need an account to use it.
  • Things only leave your device when you turn on a feature that needs it: signing in, a family key, a calendar link, connecting Google or Outlook, or using Helping Hand.
  • No ads. No selling or renting your data. No advertising or analytics trackers.
  • You can delete everything from inside the app (Settings, then Delete my data), or by emailing us. See Delete account.

Age policy

  • You must be 18 or older to set up Ai-Neut, sign in, create a family key, connect a calendar, or buy Helping Hand. The app asks you to confirm this the first time you open it (rolling out in the next app update).
  • Ai-Neut is not directed to children, and children cannot create accounts or sign in.
  • A parent can set a phone the family shares with a child to a kid's phone (under 13) or teen's phone (13 to 17) mode. These are parent controls, protected by a parent code, and they lock rooms meant for adults. On a kid's phone, what the child types is not sent to the AI model, sign-in and purchases are off, and their writing stays on that phone. Only chore check-offs sync to a linked house (see Children's information).
  • If we learn that a child under 13 has given us personal information without a parent, we delete it. Parents can email us to have it removed.

What stays on your device

These are saved in your browser's or app's local storage on your phone or computer. We don't receive them and can't see them:

  • Your journal, private notes, moods, marks, stickers, and drawings, plus the journal lock code and master code.
  • Your Ai-Neut calendar, reminders, to-dos, birthdays, and settings (theme, voice, phone mode, parent code).
  • The sign-in tokens for Google Calendar or Outlook, if you connect them, and a short cache of those events so the calendar isn't blank offline.

Microphone: the Ask/mic button uses your device's or browser's speech recognition to turn speech into text. On many Android phones and in Chrome, that service is run by Google under Google's own policies. Ai-Neut gets only the text and doesn't record or upload audio. Front camera: some themes may use the camera only as a light sensor, reading tiny frames on the device. Nothing is saved or uploaded. Both need your permission and can be turned off in your device settings.

If you tap Save a file on this phone, the app downloads a copy (Ai-Neut.json) to your device. That file is yours, and we don't receive it. Clearing the app's data, clearing the browser's site data, or uninstalling can erase what's on the device, so keep a saved file if it matters to you.

Google Calendar and Microsoft Outlook

Connecting a calendar is optional, and it's separate from signing in. Before you connect, the app explains what it will access.

  • Google Calendar asks for permission to see and edit your calendars and events (scopes calendar and calendar.events). We plan to narrow this to events plus your calendar list. Microsoft Outlook / Microsoft 365 asks for Calendars.ReadWrite, User.Read (your name and email), and offline_access (so you stay connected).
  • What we do with it: show your events in Ai-Neut's calendar views, and add, change, or delete events only when you ask. Before deleting an event, the app asks you to confirm.
  • Where it goes: your device talks directly to Google or Microsoft. Event data and access tokens are kept on your device. They are not sent to our server, not sent to the AI model unless you choose to include a specific event in a Helping Hand ask, and never used for ads.
  • Disconnect any time in Ai-Neut's Calendar settings, which removes the tokens and cached events from the device. You can also remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions or account.live.com/consent/Manage.

Google user data: Limited Use

Ai-Neut's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. In particular:

  • We use Google Calendar data only to provide the calendar features you can see in the app.
  • We don't transfer it to anyone, except as needed to provide those features with your consent, for security, to comply with the law, or as part of a merger or sale of Ai-Neut (such as the move to an LLC) after asking for your consent first.
  • We don't sell it, use it for ads, or use it to decide creditworthiness or for lending.
  • No person reads it, unless you ask us to (for example, for support), it's needed for security or to comply with the law, or it's aggregated for internal operations.
  • We don't use Google Workspace data, including Google Calendar data, to develop, improve, or train general AI or machine-learning models.

Microsoft data gets the same treatment: it's used only for the calendar features you turn on, and it's not sold, used for ads, or used to train AI models.

Helping Hand and AI

Helping Hand is the optional paid part of Ai-Neut that uses an AI model (Grok, from xAI) to answer asks, make pictures, plan a "hunt" for your town, and speak in the named voices (Luna, Ara, Ursa, Eve). When you use it, what you type or say, any picture you add, and the text to be spoken are sent to xAI so it can produce the answer. xAI processes this as our service provider under its own privacy policy and API terms. We don't let it be used for ads, and we don't sell it.

  • The journal, calendar, and the rest of the free app don't call the AI model.
  • AI answers can be wrong, so check anything important. An in-app Report button for answers and pictures is rolling out before the Google Play launch (see Safety).
  • Please don't put other people's private details, or sensitive details about a child, into an ask.

Children's information

Parents use Ai-Neut to organize family life, so you may write about your children: names, birthdays, school events, chores, or notes. That information is yours. It stays on your device unless you use a feature that sends it (a journal copy, a family key, a calendar link, or a Helping Hand ask). We use it only to provide the feature you chose, never for ads, profiling, or selling.

On a phone set to kid's phone mode, the child's writing stays on that phone (only chore check-offs sync to a linked house), the AI model is off, and sign-in, family-key setup, and purchases are off. On a teen's phone, adult rooms stay closed. Ai-Neut doesn't knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13. If you think we have, email familyainewt@gmail.com and we'll delete it.

Who else gets data

We don't sell, rent, or trade personal information, and we don't share it for advertising. We only use these service providers, and only to run Ai-Neut:

  • App hosting: the Grok app-builder platform that runs ai-neut.grok.me and stores the server data described above.
  • Website hosting: GitHub Pages and Cloudflare, for www.ai-neut.com.
  • Sign-in: Google and X, when you choose them.
  • Calendars: Google and Microsoft, directly from your device, when you connect them.
  • AI: xAI, for Helping Hand.
  • Payments: Stripe on the web, and Google Play when Helping Hand is sold in the Android app. They handle your card and send receipts.
  • Email: Google (Gmail), when you email us.

We may also share information if the law requires it, to protect someone's safety, or to protect Ai-Neut from fraud or abuse. If Ai-Neut is transferred, including to Charles's LLC, the new owner must follow this policy.

Deleting your data

In the app: Settings → Delete my data. This erases the journal and calendar from that device, and if you're signed in, it deletes your account and its server data too. On the web, or if you no longer have the app: see Delete your Ai-Neut account and data. We finish web requests within 30 days.

Your choices and rights

  • You can use Ai-Neut without an account, turn off any connection, and delete your data at any time.
  • Email us to ask for a copy of the data we hold about you, or to correct or delete it. We'll answer within 30 days, and we may need to confirm it's really you. You won't be treated differently for asking.
  • Ai-Neut is offered to users in the United States. Georgia doesn't currently have a general consumer privacy law, and we don't meet the thresholds of laws like California's CCPA, but we give these rights to everyone anyway. If you're outside the US, you may have additional rights, such as under the GDPR. Contact us to use them.

Security

All connections use HTTPS. Family sync is encrypted on your device before upload. Calendar tokens stay on your device. No system is perfectly secure, so use a phone lock, sign out of shared devices, and keep your family keys and master code private. If a breach affects your information, we'll tell you as the law requires.

Changes to this policy

We'll update the effective date when this policy changes, and for important changes (including the move to an LLC) we'll give notice in the app first. We won't use data we already have in a new way without asking you first.

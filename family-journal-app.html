A family journal that keeps the good parts in view

Most days have one good moment worth keeping. Ai-Neut's journal is built to catch it: a line, a joke, a small win at bedtime. The good stuff stays easy to find on a flat day.

Start your journal

Write a little, keep a lot

  • One true good thing, to remember
  • One ordinary good moment, saved for a flat day
  • Tonight's keep: a story, homework, or lights-out
  • A 90-day strip that counts only what was kept. Empty days are just paper, not a failure chart

A gratitude journal for the whole house

  • A five-question Sunday check-in: what held, what felt easy, what to repeat, who helped, and one small tune-up
  • A clean joke for the table, and a place for family bits
  • Small steps for kids in Grow: a word for dinner, ten minutes of play, a feeling word. No grades, no leaderboard

Private by design

  • Your journal stays on your device unless you choose to save a copy
  • No ads, no selling data, no public profiles
  • Nothing sends itself

Not therapy, just a better notebook

Ai-Neut is a journal and a family organizer. It is not therapy, counseling, or medical care. In a mental-health crisis in the US, call or text 988. See Safety.

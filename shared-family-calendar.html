A shared family calendar for busy households

Two homes, Grandma's on Tuesdays, the sitter on Fridays, and three different practice schedules. Sαréal618 puts the whole week in one place, and every linked phone sees the same plan.

Shared calendar questions

Can grandparents, a sitter, or the other household see it?

Yes. Link their phone with your family key and they see the shared plan. Each adult's journal and notes stay on their own phone.

Does everyone need an account?

You can look around without one. To share the calendar, each adult links a phone with the family key. Kids under 13 join through a parent.

Is the shared calendar free?

The shared calendar, journal, and sync between phones are part of Sαréal618's free core. Helping Hand, an optional upgrade for the AI helper and voices, is coming soon and isn't on sale yet. Nothing in the free core moves behind it.

Does Sαréal618 sync with Google Calendar?

Yes. You can paste your Google Calendar's secret iCal address today. A direct “Connect Google” sign-in is rolling out as a beta. Events and sign-in tokens stay on your device. See the Privacy Policy.

Does it work with Outlook and Apple Calendar?

Yes, through calendar links: publish an Outlook calendar and paste its ICS link, or share an iCloud calendar as a Public Calendar and paste the webcal link. Apple links are view-only and refresh every few hours. Outlook sign-in is on the way.

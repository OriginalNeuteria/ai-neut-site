Come on in.

Android phone, tablet, or Chromebook with the Play Store. Volunteer and unpaid. iPhone and iPad are not in this test. No TestFlight link yet.

At least 12 testers, opted in continuously for 14 days, before we can apply for a public release. Approval is not automatic. We will tell you when it begins.

Email support

  1. Email support@sareal618.com with the Gmail you use on Google Play.
  2. Wait for confirmation. Private opt-in and install directions come back by email. There is no public opt-in link.
  3. Stay opted in for those 14 days. Leaving and rejoining starts the count over.

The public invitation is sareal618.com/test.html. See the week.

Install and opt-in details
  • Use the Gmail that is on that device’s Play Store.
  • The opt-in steps are sent only after we confirm. They are not posted on this page.
  • Open the app when the week calls for it. A daily report is not part of the test.
  • Tell us one thing that got in the way and one idea, by email or by Send private feedback on Google Play.
  • The core app is free. Helping Hand is coming soon and is not on sale yet.

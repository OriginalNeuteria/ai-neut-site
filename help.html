Help & FAQ

What is Ai-Neut?

A journal and a family organizer. It opens on what's going right at home, with the calendar, check-ins, and house standards on the Porch. See Features.

Is it free?

Ai-Neut is free, with an optional Helping Hand upgrade. Helping Hand is an optional subscription; details are coming soon. See Pricing.

Do I need an account to look around?

No. You can open the app and look around without creating an account.

Is my family's information private?

Ai-Neut is built to be private: no public profiles, and nothing sends itself. Our full Privacy Policy is still a draft and will be finished before the Google Play launch.

What devices does it work on?

Any modern web browser on a phone, tablet, or computer today. An Android app on Google Play is coming soon.

How do I delete my account or data?

See Delete account.

Is Ai-Neut therapy or medical care?

No. Ai-Neut is not therapy, counseling, or medical care, and it is not for emergencies. In a crisis, call or text 988 (US). In an emergency, call 911.

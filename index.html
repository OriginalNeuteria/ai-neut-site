Help us test Sαréal618 on Android

Before Sαréal618 can go live on Google Play, Google needs at least 12 people testing it for 14 days. We'd love your help.

  1. Email support with the Gmail you use on Google Play. We'll send your invite and early access as soon as it's ready.
  2. Install Sαréal618 from the invite and stay in all 14 days. Open it a few times a week and try a list, an event, or the tour. Please don't uninstall early: leaving and rejoining starts your count over.
  3. Tell us one thing that bugged you and one idea. Email us, or use "Send private feedback" on Google Play.

When do the 14 days start? Once 12 or more testers are in at the same time. We'll let you know when it begins.

Device: an Android phone or tablet, or a Chromebook with the Play Store. iPhone and iPad won't work for this test.

The core app is free. Testing is volunteer and unpaid. No public review or rating is needed, just private feedback. Thank you!

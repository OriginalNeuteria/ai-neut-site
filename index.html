A little behind-the-scenes care 💜

Status: Repair in progress · Last updated Oct 28, 2026, 10:36 AM ET

Some users may see a blank screen or an unusual layout when opening Ai-Neut. We've reproduced the problem and are working on improvements to how the app loads and recovers after an interrupted connection or update.

If this happens, here's something to try: don't clear the app's data. Try a normal refresh on a reliable connection. Please don't clear app or site data or reinstall while we work on it, since some information may be saved on your device.

We'll update this notice when the repair is verified. Thanks for your patience while we make Ai-Neut more dependable. If something isn't working, please contact Ai-Neut Support.

Help us test Ai-Neut on Android

Before Ai-Neut can go live on Google Play, Google needs at least 12 people testing it for 14 days. We'd love your help.

  1. Email support@ai-neut.com with the Gmail you use on Google Play. We'll send your invite and early access as soon as it's ready.
  2. Install Ai-Neut from the invite and stay in all 14 days. Open it a few times a week and try a list, an event, or the tour. Please don't uninstall early: leaving and rejoining starts your count over.
  3. Tell us one thing that bugged you and one idea. Email us, or use "Send private feedback" on Google Play.

When do the 14 days start? Once 12 or more testers are in at the same time. We'll let you know when it begins.

Device: an Android phone or tablet, or a Chromebook with the Play Store. iPhone and iPad won't work for this test.

The core app is free, with an optional Helping Hand upgrade. Testing is volunteer and unpaid. No public review or rating is needed, just private feedback. Thank you!

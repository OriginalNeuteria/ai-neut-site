Our mission

Kids first is family first.

Ai-Neut exists to take stress out of family life, so the people in a house can grow around their kids instead of pulling against each other through them. It's for every kind of family: one parent, two homes, blended, or everyone under one roof. Stress is what turns a busy day into a fight. When the plans, the list, and a place to think are steady and in plain view, there's more room for the good stuff to grow.

Strong roots bring steady days.

Why it was made

Ai-Neut was made by a parent who couldn't find the app they needed. Most apps either keep score or try to keep you scrolling. They wanted something that helped a family stay on the same page without anyone keeping score, gave kids something fun to make, and gave grown-ups a private place to get their thoughts down before they said them out loud. It's made for parents juggling a lot, for the ones who love planning every hour, and for kids who'd rather draw than be told what to do.

What we believe

1. Cohesion independent of differences.

A family can be one home or several. What matters is that kids are steady and the adults grow around them.

2. Build, don't battle.

Ai-Neut rewards the small things that hold a house together: a checked chore, a shared plan, a moment someone laughed. It never ranks people or keeps score against anyone.

3. Head stress off early.

Seeing what's coming before it's urgent heads off arguments before they start. That's why What's due plans ahead instead of just warning you at the last minute.

4. Room to make things.

Drawing, music, writing, and building are part of a good day, not a reward you have to earn.

5. Neutral by design.

Ai-Neut organizes and points things out. It never decides for you, never takes sides, and you can leave any time and take your things with you.

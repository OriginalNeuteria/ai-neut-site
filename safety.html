Safety

Effective date: October 17, 2026. Ai-Neut is a journal and family organizer for adults. This page explains how we protect children and families, how to report a problem, and where to get help.

Need help now?

  • Danger or emergency: call 911 (US).
  • Mental-health or suicide crisis: call or text 988, or chat at 988lifeline.org.
  • Child abuse concerns: Childhelp, 1-800-422-4453 (call or text).
  • Domestic violence: National Domestic Violence Hotline, 1-800-799-7233, or text START to 88788.
  • To report the online sexual exploitation of a child: CyberTipline (NCMEC), 1-800-843-5678.

Ai-Neut can't respond to emergencies, and nobody monitors what you write.

Our child safety standards

  • Zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE). Ai-Neut prohibits any content that sexualizes, exploits, or endangers children, including child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming, and sextortion. This applies to everything people make or share in Ai-Neut, including Helping Hand AI asks and pictures.
  • Helping Hand is set up to refuse requests for sexual content involving minors and for sexualized pictures of real people, using xAI's safety systems and our own instructions to the model.
  • If we become aware of apparent CSAM, we remove it, preserve what the law requires, and report it to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and to law enforcement as required by US law (18 U.S.C. § 2258A). We cooperate with law enforcement.
  • Ai-Neut has no public profiles, no public chat, and no way to message strangers. Family sharing works only between phones you link with a one-time family key.
  • Ai-Neut is for adults 18+. Children can't create accounts. Parents can set a shared phone to kid's phone (under 13) or teen's phone (13 to 17) mode with a parent code. Those modes keep adult rooms closed, and on a kid's phone the AI model, sign-in, and purchases are off.
  • Adults-only rooms (such as adult humor in Laugh) open only on a parent phone, after you confirm you're 18 or older, and never appear on kid or teen phones.

Reporting a problem

  • In the app (rolling out before the Google Play launch): every Helping Hand answer and picture has a Report option (flag icon). Choose a reason and send it without leaving the app. You can also use Settings → Report a problem.
  • By email: familyainewt@gmail.com, subject "Safety report". Please don't attach illegal images. Describe what you saw instead.
  • We read every report, reply to safety reports within 2 business days, and use them to improve Ai-Neut's filters and instructions to the model.

Child safety point of contact

Charles-Gordon Stuart Eaton, developer of Ai-Neut, familyainewt@gmail.com, Johns Creek, Georgia, USA.

Laws we follow

We follow applicable child safety and privacy laws, including the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and federal CSAM reporting requirements. See our Privacy Policy for how children's information is handled, and our Terms for acceptable use.

Looking for a Cozi alternative? Here's an honest comparison.

Cozi is a well-known, mature family organizer, and for a lot of families it's the right pick. Ai-Neut is newer and built around a different idea: a journal and a family organizer that opens on what's going right. Here's how they differ, so you can choose.

Choose Cozi if…

  • Recipes, meal planning, and shopping mode are the center of your week
  • You want a long-established app with iPhone and Android apps today

Try Ai-Neut if…

  • You want a family calendar and a family journal in one place
  • You juggle school deadlines and want them as a readable list
  • You co-parent, and want an app that keeps the kid's week in front instead of the conflict
  • You'd rather not see ads (Ai-Neut has none, on any plan)
  • You have a high school or college student juggling online-class deadlines

Trying both

You don't have to switch everything at once. Keep your current app as it is, and paste any calendar link you already use (school, team, Google, Outlook, or iCloud) into Ai-Neut's Calendar settings, so both stay in view while you decide.

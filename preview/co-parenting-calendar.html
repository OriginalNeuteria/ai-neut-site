A co-parenting calendar that keeps the kid's week in front

Two homes, one kid, one plan. Ai-Neut gives both households the same calendar for pickups, practices, school deadlines, and weekends, without turning the app into a scoreboard.

Start a family calendar

The schedule, shared

  • One calendar both homes can see, linked with a family key
  • Repeating weekends and handoffs, such as a 1st, 3rd, and 5th weekend schedule
  • Ride and childcare swaps, and who's driving
  • School deadlines from D2L, Canvas, or Blackboard in the Due view, so both homes know what's due

Calmer messages

  • Before a hard text, a gentle check: “Will the kid feel this?”
  • Three ways to say it: warm, direct, or your voice
  • Nothing sends itself. You choose what goes out

Feed the right wolf

  • The app opens on what's going right: a clean joke, tonight's keep, a good moment saved for a flat day
  • The planning lives on the Porch, so opening the app doesn't put the hard part in your hand
  • Single, blended, or co-parenting, every family gets the same front door

What Ai-Neut is not

Ai-Neut isn't a court tool, a legal record, or a way to keep score between adults. It isn't legal advice, therapy, or counseling. If you need court-ready records or a court-ordered communication app, use the tool your court or attorney names. Ai-Neut is for the everyday week the kid actually lives in. See our Terms and Safety page.

Co-parenting calendar questions

Is there a free co-parenting calendar?

Ai-Neut's shared calendar, journal, and family sync are part of the free core, with an optional Helping Hand upgrade ($36 a year) for the AI helper and voices. Nothing in the free plan moves behind it.

Does my co-parent need an account?

You can look around without one. To share the calendar, each adult links a phone with the family key.

Can it stay private from each other?

Yes. The shared calendar is shared. Each adult's journal and notes stay on their own phone.
